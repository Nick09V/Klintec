## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered for mission-critical B2B enterprise procurement, facility management, and complex labor-cost modeling adhering to strict legal frameworks (such as FEDELIMP standards and Ecuador's IESS labor mandates). It projects institutional trust, fiscal exactness, algorithmic transparency, and clinical reliability.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
The system adopts an **Engineered Corporate Minimalist** paradigm, blending high-density technical layouts with purposeful micro-contrast:
- **Clinical Rigor:** High visual hygiene, eliminating non-functional embellishments to prioritize massive multi-variable calculation sheets, compliance audits, and legal breakdown formulas.
- **Architectural Utility:** Crisp surface tiers, subtle hairline borders, and precise visual anchors that give operations managers and financial directors instant clarity across high-stakes tenders.
- **Controlled Density:** Balanced compact views for desktop bidding engines, allowing multi-column cost matrices to sit comfortably alongside regulatory validation widgets.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative, enterprise-grade atmosphere rooted in structural navy tones, clean technical blues, and purposeful sanitary teals.

### Functional Palette Matrix
- **Primary Navy (`#0F172A`, `#1E3A8A`):** Anchors brand architecture, navigation mastheads, structural containers, and primary authority markers.
- **Secondary Cyan (`#0284C7`):** Powers key operational actions, interactive links, primary data points, and forward-stage progression buttons.
- **Tertiary Sanitary Teal (`#0D9488`):** Denotes facility hygiene benchmarks, environmental safety standards, certified chemical handling, and verified process passes.
- **Neutral Slate Surfaces:**
  - `Canvas Canvas`: `#F8FAFC` (Cool technical base)
  - `Surface Layer 1`: `#FFFFFF` (Card surfaces, data grids)
  - `Surface Layer 2`: `#F1F5F9` (Nested panels, table headers, calculation wells)
  - `Borders & Rules`: `#E2E8F0` (Default separation), `#CBD5E1` (Focused structural frames)
  - `Text Neutral`: `#334155` (Body, calculation keys), `#0F172A` (Headings, primary metrics)

### Regulatory & Bid Competitiveness Semaphores
- **Compliant / Winning Index (`#10B981`):** Optimal margin, 100% IESS statutory coverage, certified labor compliance.
- **Margin Alert / Revision Required (`#F59E0B`):** Borderline utility margin (<8%), unconfirmed holiday surcharges, missing auxiliary equipment costs.
- **Non-Compliant / Critical Loss (`#EF4444`):** Legal wage deficit, violation of sector-specific minimums, unviable operational bid.

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances executive authority with tabular accuracy:

- **Executive Hierarchy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clean geometry and balanced open counters for high-level headings, bidding overview decks, and enterprise section indicators.
- **Workhorse Clarity (`Inter`):** Renders all regulatory clauses, specifications, contract guidelines, and administrative controls with extreme legibility at dense standard sizes.
- **Tabular & Financial Data (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated exclusively to monetary values (USD), surface metrics ($m^2$, linear meters), shift calculations, overtime multipliers, and statutory IESS deductions. Fixed numerical widths guarantee visual vertical alignment across complex multi-row estimates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt structural module governed by a **12-column fluid grid** system optimized for high-density enterprise displays:

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12 columns, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `2rem` (32px) outer canvas margins. Multi-column calculation tables take a 9-column span, paired with a persistent 3-column real-time regulatory summary sidebar.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `1rem` (16px) gutters, `1.5rem` (24px) margins. Sidebars collapse into docked bottom sheets or tabbed auxiliary drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `0.75rem` (12px) gutters, `1rem` (16px) margins. Strict stacked layouts prioritizing core total cost and compliance badges.

### Layout Principles
- Use compact vertical rhythm (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) inside formula matrices, cost break-up tables, and audit logs.
- Preserve generous outer panel spacing (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) around high-level KPI cards and RFP stage transitions to prevent visual fatigue during marathon bidding cycles.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system relies on **low-contrast architectural outlines and precise tonal layering** over heavy drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (App Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` base layer.
- **Tier 1 (Cards, Modules, Tables):** Solid `#FFFFFF` elevated via a crisp `1px solid #E2E8F0` border.
- **Tier 2 (Sticky Headers, Active Modals, Flyouts):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an ultra-diffused technical shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Competitive Validation Tooltips & Critical Overlays):** `#0F172A` high-contrast dark surface with `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.25)`.

### Border Integrity
Separations between calculation fields, summary cells, and status indicators strictly employ 1px hairlines (`#E2E8F0` or `#CBD5E1`). Zero-radius table dividers ensure data lines remain uninterrupted and precise.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a **Soft / Engineering Corner Radius** (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Micro-radii (4px / 0.25rem):** Form inputs, table cells, metric badges, and interactive controls. This low curvature maximizes usable horizontal data density in wide spreadsheets.
- **Mid-radii (8px / 0.5rem):** KPI insight panels, module envelopes, and modal windows.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Restricted solely to status pills, certification badges (e.g., "IESS Validated", "FEDELIMP A-Grade"), and competitive index markers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Operational:** Deep navy background (`#0F172A`), white text, 4px radius, 36px default height. Hover states lighten subtly to `#1E293B`.
- **Secondary Technical:** Clear background, `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` text. Hover prompts `#F1F5F9`.
- **Tertiary Sanitary Action:** Solid `#0D9488` with `#FFFFFF` text for hygiene protocol attachments and specialized equipment provisioning.

### Input Fields & Calculation Cells
- **Text Inputs:** Height 36px, 1px border `#CBD5E1`, background `#FFFFFF`. Active state uses a 1px ring in `#0284C7` with no heavy outer glow.
- **Numeric Quoting Inputs:** JetBrains Mono font, right-aligned numbers, with static prefix labels (e.g., `$`, `m²`, `hrs/mes`) rendered in muted `#64748B`.

### KPI & Metric Cards
- Plain white containers bordered with hairline `#E2E8F0`.
- Top row: Micro-label in uppercase tracking (`label-caps`), paired with contextual state icon.
- Center: Prominent numerical value in `mono-metric-lg`.
- Bottom: Competitiveness delta indicator with green/amber/red status pill showing deviation from standard FEDELIMP pricing ceilings.

### Checkboxes & Regulatory Switches
- **Checkboxes:** 16px square, 3px corner radius, bold checkmark in white on active `#0284C7`.
- **Compliance Toggles:** Used for statutory options (e.g., "Decimotercero mensualizado", "Fondo de Reserva"). Subtle 20px height track, high contrast knob.

### Facility Breakdown Data Tables
- Header row in `#F1F5F9`, 11px uppercase bold labels.
- Alternate rows remain plain white to avoid visual noise; row-hover switches to `#F8FAFC`.
- Pinned bottom row for real-time mathematical aggregation: Gross Labor Cost + Social Benefits (IESS 12.15% patronal) + Chemical Amortization + Overhead + Industrial Margin.
